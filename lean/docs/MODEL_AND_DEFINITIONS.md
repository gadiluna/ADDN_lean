# Model and definitions

This guide maps the model of [Computation in Anonymous Directed Dynamic Networks: Exact Worst-Case Termination Time and Network-Wise Intractability](../../paper.tex) to its Lean definitions. Each declaration link opens the defining source line. The [paper–Lean correspondence table](PAPER_LEAN_CORRESPONDENCE.md) separately maps the numbered results to their theorem statements and proofs.

## Distributed networks and local computation

These definitions implement Section 2, “Model and History Trees.” The physical population and graph are parameters of the execution semantics; they are not arguments supplied to the local protocol.

| Paper concept | Lean definitions | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Population and dynamic directed multigraph | [Counting.Admissible](../Counting/Network.lean#L41) | A finite agent type `V` and a sequence `G : Nat → V → V → Nat`. The value `G t u v` is the number of arcs from `u` to `v` in round `t`; zero means no arc. Loops and parallel arcs are permitted. Admissibility requires a positive leader count, exactly that many leaders, and strong connectivity in every positive round. |
| Strong connectivity | [DirectedCounting.StronglyConnected](../DirectedCountingFormalization/Connectivity.lean#L15), [Counting.CutConnected](../Counting/Network.lean#L9) | Reachability between every ordered pair of agents; equivalently, an outgoing arc from every nonempty proper subset. Reflexive reachability admits a singleton with no arcs. |
| Leaders and their known count | [Counting.leaderCount](../Counting/Network.lean#L37), [Counting.Protocol](../Counting/Protocol.lean#L29) | The physical assignment is `leader : V → Bool`. Each agent initializes from the common count and its own role; agent identities and the true population are absent from this interface. |
| Sending the previous state and late outdegree awareness | [Counting.incoming](../Counting/Protocol.lean#L54), [Counting.Protocol.advance](../Counting/Protocol.lean#L35), [Counting.Protocol.trace](../Counting/Protocol.lean#L89) | Received messages are the multiset of previous sender states, weighted by arc multiplicity. The local update then receives that multiset and the current outdegree. |
| Silent halting | [Counting.Status](../Counting/Protocol.lean#L20), [Counting.Status.message](../Counting/Protocol.lean#L25), [Counting.Protocol.advance](../Counting/Protocol.lean#L35) | A halted state retains its output, sends no message, and remains unchanged. The execution filters messages from halted senders. |
| Correctness of Counting | [Counting.Protocol.SolvesCounting](../Counting/Network.lean#L54) | Safety and eventual termination are required for every finite admissible population and leader assignment: every produced answer is the population size, and all agents eventually halt with that answer. |

## History trees and vistas

Natural level `0` represents the initial leader-status partition. The paper's common root at level −1 is treated separately or implicitly; it is not the initial local history. Expanded histories, visible quotient nodes, and stored DAGs are distinct representations, connected by the theorems listed below.

| Paper concept | Lean definitions | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full observation history | [Counting.FullHistory](../Counting/Protocol.lean#L50), [Counting.history](../Counting/Protocol.lean#L73), [Counting.Protocol.decode](../Counting/Protocol.lean#L82) | A recursive mathematical record for the hypothetical always-active full-information execution: previous history, current outdegree, and incoming multiset. Decoding a protocol filters messages from senders that it has halted. This specification is not the executable storage representation. |
| Indistinguishability classes and history-tree nodes | [DirectedCounting.sameMultigraphHistory](../DirectedCountingFormalization/MultigraphHistoryModel.lean#L64), [DirectedCounting.PaperHistory.Node](../DirectedCountingFormalization/PaperHistory.lean#L21), [DirectedCounting.PaperHistory.classOf](../DirectedCountingFormalization/PaperHistory.lean#L29) | Nodes at a level are quotient classes of agents with equal observation histories. The recursive relation compares previous classes, outdegrees, and incoming counts from each previous class. |
| Fibers and anonymities | [DirectedCounting.PaperHistory.fiber](../DirectedCountingFormalization/PaperHistory.lean#L31), [DirectedCounting.PaperHistory.anonymity](../DirectedCountingFormalization/PaperHistory.lean#L35) | A node's fiber is its set of physical agents; its anonymity is the cardinality of that fiber. These are proof data and are not stored as observations in a vista. |
| Black-parent edges and initial labels | [DirectedCounting.PaperHistory.parent](../DirectedCountingFormalization/PaperHistory.lean#L109), [DirectedCounting.PaperHistory.label](../DirectedCountingFormalization/PaperHistory.lean#L180) | The parent forgets the final round. The initial label records the leader role when instantiated with the Counting input. |
| Outdegree labels and red multiplicities | [DirectedCounting.PaperHistory.outdegreeLabel](../DirectedCountingFormalization/PaperHistory.lean#L216), [DirectedCounting.PaperHistory.redMultiplicity](../DirectedCountingFormalization/PaperHistory.lean#L220) | The child class has a common current outdegree. A red multiplicity counts arcs from one previous fiber to each recipient in a given child fiber. |
| Causal support and visible nodes | [Counting.causalSupport](../Counting/Vista.lean#L10), [Counting.visibleNodes](../Counting/Vista.lean#L16), [Counting.VistaIsomorphism.ActualNode](../Counting/VistaIsomorphismActual.lean#L10) | Visible nodes are quotient classes whose fibers intersect the backward causal support of the observer. `ActualNode` restricts them to levels at or before the observation round. Visibility does not require the entire fiber to belong to the causal support. |
| Vistas up to isomorphism | [Counting.VistaIsomorphism.ActualIso](../Counting/VistaIsomorphismActual.lean#L63) | Levelwise bijections preserve initial labels, outdegrees, black-parent edges, red multiplicities, and the distinguished bottom node. Physical agent names and fiber sizes are not observable labels. |
| Intrinsic vista obtained from a local history | [Counting.TimingProtocolLocal.levels](../Counting/TimingProtocolLocal.lean#L27), [Counting.VistaIsomorphism.Node](../Counting/VistaIsomorphism.lean#L98), [Counting.VistaIsomorphism.Iso](../Counting/VistaIsomorphism.lean#L102) | The distinct histories in the backward closure form a layered vista independently of a choice of physical population. A full actual level is expressed by `visibleNodes ... = Finset.univ`. |
| Executable shared DAG | [Counting.Executable.Node](../Counting/Executable.lean#L43), [Counting.Executable.Vista](../Counting/Executable.lean#L51), [Counting.Executable.Vista.denotes](../Counting/DagSemantics.lean#L82), [Counting.Executable.VistaSyntax](../Counting/DagSemanticsSyntax.lean#L36) | The stored representation is a node array with parent indices, labels, sparse counted incoming references, and a bottom index. `denotes` interprets its bottom history. Local indices name DAG nodes, not network agents. Syntactic validity is distinct from network realizability. |

The representation correspondences are proved explicitly:

- [history_eq_iff_sameMultigraphHistory](../Counting/HistoryBridge.lean#L27) equates observation-history equality with the quotient-class relation.
- [levels_eq_image](../Counting/TimingProtocolLocal.lean#L147) identifies the intrinsic visible levels with the actual visible quotient nodes.
- [actual_history_eq_iff_actualIso](../Counting/VistaIsomorphismActual.lean#L162) equates history equality with isomorphism of actual vistas, including across different populations. [ActualIso.same_protocol_status](../Counting/VistaIsomorphismActual.lean#L173) transfers this to every protocol, including silent halting.
- [decodedLevel_eq_levels](../Counting/DagSemanticsView.lean#L95) and [physical_vista_run_correct](../Counting/DagSemanticsPhysical.lean#L101) connect valid stored DAGs and their iterated local updates to the semantic vistas and actual all-active histories.

## Realizability and complexity

The mathematical recognition predicates correspond to Section 4 and Appendices E–F. Their binary encodings specify total decision problems, including malformed inputs.

| Paper concept | Lean definitions | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Realization and order spectrum | [Counting.Realizability.Realizable](../Counting/RealizabilityComplete.lean#L13), [Counting.Realizability.spectrum](../Counting/RealizabilitySpectrum.lean#L9) | An order is compatible when some admissible network with that population and leader count produces the specified observer history, hence the specified vista up to the proved isomorphism. |
| Mathematical recognition problems | [Counting.Realizability.AtMostOneVista](../Counting/RealizabilityRecognition.lean#L11), [Counting.Realizability.SomeAgentKnows](../Counting/RealizabilityRecognition.lean#L63) | The first predicate requires at most one compatible order. The second requires an admissible network and an observer whose history satisfies that predicate. |
| Recognition on binary inputs | [Counting.Encoding.BinaryAtMostOneVista](../Counting/EncodingRecognition.lean#L138), [Counting.Encoding.binaryVistaSpectrum](../Counting/EncodingRecognition.lean#L133), [Counting.Realizability.encodedSpectrum](../Counting/RealizabilityKnowledge.lean#L39), [Counting.Encoding.BinarySomeAgentKnows](../Counting/EncodingNetworkRecognition.lean#L6) | Numerical labels and multiplicities are encoded in binary. Malformed vistas have empty spectrum; invalid network encodings or inadmissible networks are negative instances of `SomeAgentKnows`. |
| Machine and computation steps | [Counting.Machine.Symbol](../Counting/MachineModel.lean#L11), [Counting.Machine.Program](../Counting/MachineModel.lean#L24), [Counting.Machine.step](../Counting/MachineModel.lean#L78), [Counting.Machine.HaltsAt](../Counting/MachineModel.lean#L148) | Programs have finite control and a five-symbol tape alphabet. Each write or unit head movement is one transition. [step_simulates](../Counting/MachineModel.lean#L85) connects each step to mathlib's `Turing.TM0`. |
| Polynomial time and decision | [Counting.Complexity.polynomial](../Counting/ComplexityModel.lean#L15), [Counting.Complexity.RunsIn](../Counting/ComplexityModel.lean#L17), [Counting.Complexity.DecidesIn](../Counting/ComplexityModel.lean#L20), [Counting.Complexity.P](../Counting/ComplexityModel.lean#L43) | A fixed machine must halt on every binary input within a bound `coefficient * (input.length + 1)^exponent`; decision additionally requires agreement with language membership. |
| NP and coNP | [Counting.Complexity.NP](../Counting/ComplexityModel.lean#L48), [Counting.Complexity.coNP](../Counting/ComplexityModel.lean#L55) | NP uses polynomial-length binary certificates and a deterministic verifier that runs in polynomial time on every encoded input. coNP is NP membership of the complementary language. |
| Polynomial reductions and completeness | [Counting.Complexity.ComputesCleanIn](../Counting/ComplexityModel.lean#L31), [Counting.Complexity.ManyOnePoly](../Counting/ComplexityModel.lean#L57), [Counting.Complexity.NPHard](../Counting/ComplexityModel.lean#L62), [Counting.Complexity.NPComplete](../Counting/ComplexityModel.lean#L63), [Counting.Complexity.coNPHard](../Counting/ComplexityModel.lean#L64), [Counting.Complexity.coNPComplete](../Counting/ComplexityModel.lean#L65) | A many-one reduction includes a concrete polynomial-time machine computing the reduction function and leaving a clean output tape, together with preservation of membership. Hardness quantifies over all languages in the corresponding class. |
| Local runtime of Counting | [Counting.Machine.CountingRuntime.LocalRuntimeBound](../Counting/MachineCountingRuntimeTheorems.lean#L15) | Counts machine transitions for initialization and every live local round. Each round starts with the encoded local state, binary-compressed received messages, and learned outdegree already supplied. Raw packet collection and preparation of that external input tape are outside this local runtime interface. |

## Logical assumptions and verification

The definitions above specify the formal model; they are not additional mathematical axioms. Lean checks proofs relative to these definitions and their stated hypotheses. Assessing their correspondence with the paper is a separate, inspectable part of the review.

The [transitive axiom audit](../Counting/AxiomAudit.lean#L7) permits only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`, and rejects `sorryAx`, added mathematical axioms, and native-evaluation axioms. The [verification guide](VALIDATION.md) gives the build and audit procedure. The [scope](CURRENT_SCOPE.md) identifies the numbered formal statements covered, and the [correspondence table](PAPER_LEAN_CORRESPONDENCE.md) links their complete Lean theorem statements.
